What does this module solve?

Default behavior

By default, working with email in odoo is based on the configuration of campaign mailboxes. Which are not tied to a specific user. For example sales@mycompany.com , projects@mycompany.com. And the address that we see in the letter "from" is look like individual, but in fact it's just text for display, the letter will actually be sent to the campaign's email mailbox.

Next, odoo's internal routing distributes incoming emails inside odoo. This means working with email inside odoo, without individual mailboxes and even email clients.

Behavior after install

Incoming and outgoing mail servers inside odoo are linked to a specific user. For example mike@mycompany.com and the emails are sent from this individual server, and the email address of this user (res.user->partner_id->email) is also indicated in the "from" header. And in "from" we see real email address. And the letter will actually be sent to this email address.

Thus, the letter is also created in the "sent" in the mailbox and can be seen by any mail client. And the customer, making a reply, also responds to a specific user and the letter is attached to the desired record, for example, an sale order.

And it also appears in the incoming message as not read (by default, odoo makes all emails read). Meanwhile, the employee of your campaign can fully work both in an email client such as Google, or also work inside odoo. This scheme is simpler and more understandable for working with email

In this scheme, employees have the opportunity to use all the functions and capabilities of any email clients. At the same time, it also remains possible to correspond and save history inside odoo.
